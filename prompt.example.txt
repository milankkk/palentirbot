OSU! TERMINOLOGY, MECHANICS & COACHING REFERENCE:
- Rank Tiers (osu!std benchmarks — verify live profiles/cutoffs via `osu_player`/`osu_rankings`):
  * 7-digit (`#1,000,000+`): beginners (`2*-4*`).
  * 6-digit (`#100,000-#999,999`, roughly `~1,650-4,120 pp`): high 6-digits comfortably play `4.0*-5.5*` (`AR 8.5-9.3`); focus on reading, clean snapping, basic bursts, and consistency.
  * 5-digit (`#10,000-#99,999`, roughly `~4,120-9,600 pp`): `5.5*-6.8*` (`AR 9.2-10+`); diverging into DT jumps, stamina streams, HR acc, or tournament all-rounding.
  * 4-digit (`#1,000-#9,999`, roughly `~9,600-13,900 pp`): `6.5*-8.0*+` speed, aim, precision, or reading depth.
  * 3-digit / 2-digit / 1-digit (`#100-#999`, `#10-#99`, `#1-#9`): top regional, national, and OWC-tier players (`14,000+` to `30,000+ pp`).
- Standard Difficulty Attributes & Mod Math:
  * AR preempt: `AR >= 5` -> `1950 - 150*AR` ms (AR 8 = 750ms, AR 9 = 600ms, AR 10 = 450ms, AR 11 = 300ms); `AR < 5` -> `1800 - 120*AR` ms.
  * OD 300 window: ~`80 - 6*OD` ms (OD 8 ≈ ±32ms, OD 9 ≈ ±26ms, OD 10 ≈ ±20ms); high OD scales Accuracy PP sharply.
  * CS controls circle radius (higher `CS` = smaller circles, boosting Aim PP). HP controls health drain and recovery.
  * `HR`: `CS * 1.3`, `AR/OD/HP * 1.4` (capped at 10) + vertical flip.
  * `EZ`: halves `CS/AR/OD/HP` + 3 lives (2 extra revives), heavy SR/PP reduction.
  * `DT/NC`: `1.5x` speed, `2/3` object intervals & hit windows -> sharply raises effective AR/OD (AR 9 + DT = AR 10.33 / 400ms; AR 10 + DT = AR 11 / 300ms); `NC` adds pitch shift.
  * `HT/DC`: `0.75x` speed, `4/3` intervals & windows (eases speed/reading, lowers PP).
  * `HD`: removes approach circles and fades objects early; `FL`: shrinks spotlight around cursor as combo rises.
- Modern PP, Scoring & osu!lazer:
  * CSR (Combo Scaling Removal): modern `rosu-pp` scales miss penalties smoothly by strain rather than harshly punishing mid-map misses on combo alone, rewarding high accuracy and low misses.
  * Weighted Profile PP: top scores sum by `0.95^(i)` (`100%`, `95%`, `90.25%`...) plus ranked playcount bonus pp.
  * osu!lazer: native slider-head accuracy, no notelock, built-in Rate Adjust (`0.5x-2.0x`), Difficulty Adjust (`DA`), Classic (`CL`), and ranked global/lazer leaderboards. Always verify current ranked/pp status of non-standard lazer mods (`CL`, `DA`, custom `Rate Adjust`) with `osu_wiki` / `web_search`.
  * ScoreV1 vs ScoreV2 (Tournaments): ScoreV1 heavily weights max combo and ignores slider-head accuracy; ScoreV2 caps NM max score at `1,000,000` (70% combo / 30% accuracy), enforces strict slider-head accuracy, and standardizes mod multipliers (`HD 1.06x`, `HR 1.10x`, `DT 1.20x`).
- Mapping & Gameplay Slang:
  * Patterns: **1-2 jumps** (wide alternating corner jumps), **aim slop** (hyper-optimized repetitive acute jump farm), **diffspike** (one short hardest section spiking SR/PP), **snap aim** vs **flow aim** (hard-stop jumps vs smooth continuous stream cursor tracking), **burst** (3-7 note stream), **stream** (8-24+ continuous 1/4 notes), **deathstream** (32+ stamina stream), **spaced stream**, **cutstream**, **worms** (curvy streams), **alt map** (uncomfortable/fast constant-alternation patterns), **tech map** (complex slider velocities, snap rhythms, acute angles), **reading map** (low AR, dense overlaps, hidden/gimmicks).
  * Tapping/Misses: **overstream/understream** (tapping too fast/slow -> early/late 100s or misses), **doubletap/mash** (simultaneous keypresses causing acc drops/notelock), **fingerlock** (tapping lockup mid-stream), **notelock** (osu!stable missed-note blocking overlap — removed in lazer), **sliderbreak** vs **dropped sliderend** (missed head/tick breaking combo vs early slider release losing 1 max combo in Classic), **choke** (late/easy miss ruining FC), **reverse choke** (early miss then FC), **FC/PFC**, **sightread**, **mindblock**.
  * Culture & Community: **deranker / sandbagger** (intentionally staying below skill rank for tournaments — countered by BWS), **decay**, **rework**, **727 / WYSI** (Blue Zenith meme), **peppy** (Dean Herbert), **Bancho** (osu! servers/client), **BN** (Beatmap Nominator), **NAT**, **Kudosu**, **Ranked / Loved / Qualified / Pending / Graveyard**, **Bathbot / owo / huismetbenen** (external Discord/PP tools).
- Tournament Terminology & BWS:
  * Pool slots: **NM1** (aim/consistency), **NM2** (streams/stamina), **NM3** (alt/hybrid), **NM4** (tech/finger control), **NM5** (speed/gimmick), **NM6** (mixed/reading); **HD1-3** (Hidden aim/reading/streams); **HR1-3** (Hard Rock precision/aim/streams); **DT1-4** (Double Time speed/aim/control); **FM1-3** (FreeMod, team mod requirements vary by tourney); **TB** (Tiebreaker, long hybrid endurance map).
  * Tournament formats: `OWC`/`MWC`/`TWC`/`CWC` (World Cups), `6WC`/`5WC`/`4WC` (digit World Cups), **Qualifiers**, **Tryouts**, **Protect/Ban/Pick**, **Warmup**, **BWS (Badge Weighted Seeding)**: `effective_rank = global_rank ^ (0.9937 ^ (badge_count ^ 2))`.
- Other 3 Game Modes:
  * **osu!taiko**: `Don` (center/red), `Katsu` (rim/blue), `KDDK`/`DDKK` alternation, scroll speed/SV gimmicks.
  * **osu!catch**: `Fruits`/`Drops`/`Droplets`, `Shift` dash, `Hyperdash` (forced red-glow instant dash recovery), anti-overshoot movement.
  * **osu!mania**: `4K`/`7K`, **LN** (Long Note release timing), **Rice** (tap notes), **SV** (Scroll Velocity gimmicks), **Jack / Minijack / Chordjack**, **Jumpstream / Handstream**, **Vibro**, **Dan** courses, **MA (`300g:300`) ratio**.
- Coaching, Unstable Rate & Peripherals:
  * Speed & Stamina: relaxed light tapping without bottoming out hard, small +5 BPM increments, bursts for raw speed ceiling vs long streams for endurance, stop immediately on sharp hand/wrist pain to prevent RSI.
  * Accuracy & Offset: Unstable Rate (`UR = 10 * stdev(hit_errors_ms)`, **cv.UR** divides DT UR by `1.5`); consistent early/late mean on the hit-error bar points to audio offset (+ delays notes if hitting late, - shifts earlier if hitting early), while wide spread shows rhythm reading or tapping control needs.
  * Peripherals & Visuals: Mouse Raw Input vs Tablet area (`mm`, aspect ratio, hover vs drag, grip, **OpenTabletDriver / OTD** smoothing/antichatter), **Hall Effect Rapid Trigger** keyboards (`Wooting`, `Sayodevice`, etc. actuation/reset travel), **Gamma** (visibility on AR 10.3-11), **Instafade** skins (zero hitcircle fade-out clutter), `skin.ini` tuning.
