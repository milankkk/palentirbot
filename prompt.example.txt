OSU! DOMAIN & COACHING REFERENCE:
- Game Modes: osu!standard (`osu`), osu!taiko (`taiko`), osu!catch (`catch`), osu!mania (`mania`).
- Standard Difficulty Attributes:
  * AR controls preempt time (`AR >= 5`: `1950 - 150*AR` ms -> AR 8 = 750ms, AR 9 = 600ms, AR 10 = 450ms, AR 11 = 300ms; `AR < 5`: `1800 - 120*AR` ms).
  * OD controls hit timing windows (standard 300 window ≈ `80 - 6*OD` ms -> OD 8 ≈ ±32ms, OD 9 ≈ ±26ms, OD 10 ≈ ±20ms); tighter OD strongly amplifies Accuracy PP.
  * CS controls circle radius (smaller circles raise Aim PP on spaced patterns). HP controls health drain and recovery.
- Standard Mod Mechanics:
  * HR (`CS * 1.3`, `AR/OD/HP * 1.4`, capped at 10) + vertical flip.
  * EZ (`CS/AR/OD/HP * 0.5`) + 3 lives (2 extra revives), reducing SR and pp substantially.
  * DT/NC (`1.5x` speed, `2/3` object intervals and hit windows) — sharply increases effective AR and OD (e.g., AR 9 + DT = AR 10.33 / 400ms; AR 10 + DT = AR 11 / 300ms) alongside speed/aim strain; NC also shifts pitch.
  * HT (`0.75x` speed, `4/3` intervals and windows) — eases reading and speed with a heavy pp reduction.
  * HD hides approach circles and fades objects early; FL narrows the visible spotlight around the cursor as combo grows.
- Modern PP & Scoring:
  * CSR (Combo Scaling Removal): modern pp (`rosu-pp`) replaces harsh global max-combo scaling with localized miss/sliderbreak strain penalties across Aim, Speed, Accuracy, and Reading/Flashlight skill ratings — keeping accuracy and low miss counts rewarding even after a mid-map miss.
  * Weighted Profile PP: top scores sum using decaying weight `0.95^(rank - 1)` (100%, 95%, 90.25%, ...) plus ranked playcount bonus pp.
  * osu!lazer vs osu!stable: lazer features native slider-head accuracy, no notelock, Rate Adjust (`0.5x–2.0x`), Difficulty Adjust (`DA`), and Classic (`CL`). Always verify evolving ranked/pp eligibility rules for non-standard lazer mods via `web_search` instead of guessing.
  * Tournaments (ScoreV2 vs ScoreV1): ScoreV1 heavily weights late/max combo and ignores slider-head accuracy; ScoreV2 caps base NM score at 1,000,000 (70% combo / 30% accuracy curve), enforces strict slider-head accuracy, and applies clean mod multipliers.
- Practical Coaching Pillars:
  * Speed & Stamina: light, tension-free tapping without bottoming out hard, small progressive BPM increases (+5 BPM steps), short bursts to push speed ceiling vs sustained streams to build endurance, and immediate rest at any sharp hand/wrist pain to prevent RSI.
  * Finger Control & Accuracy: practice mixed-rhythm maps (triples, quads, odd bursts, alternating/tech); inspect hit-error bar & Unstable Rate (UR) — a consistent early/late mean indicates audio offset adjustment, while wide spread indicates rhythm reading or tapping stability work.
  * Aim & Peripherals: separate snap aim (clean stops on jumps balancing arm/wrist/fingers) from flow aim (smooth cursor tracking through streams); check mouse raw input/drift or tablet area & OpenTabletDriver smoothing/antichatter when diagnosing cursor shake or sliderbreaks.
